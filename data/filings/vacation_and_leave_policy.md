# Vacation and Leave Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days of PTO per month, totaling 15 days per year. PTO accrual increases to 20 days annually after 3 years of continuous employment, and 25 days after 6 years.

## Carryover Rules
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any PTO balance beyond this cap is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is accrued separately from PTO at a rate of 1 day per month, up to 12 days annually. Sick leave does not carry over and resets each calendar year.

## Parental Leave
NimbusCloud Inc. provides 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, applicable to birth, adoption, or foster placement.

## Requesting Time Off
PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal for approval, except in cases of emergency. Manager approval is required for all PTO requests exceeding 5 consecutive days.
