# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose
This document outlines the process and limits for reimbursable business expenses at NimbusCloud Inc.

## Submission Process
All expense reports must be submitted through the Expensify platform within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by Finance.

## Reimbursable Categories
- Client meals: up to $75 per person
- Domestic travel: economy class airfare, standard hotel rates up to $250/night
- International travel: requires pre-approval from department VP
- Software/tools: up to $200/month without additional approval; above this requires manager sign-off

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, alcohol at non-client events, and traffic/parking violations are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## Approval Chain
Expenses under $500 require single manager approval. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require both manager and Finance approval. Expenses above $5,000 require VP-level sign-off.

## Corporate Card Usage
Employees issued a corporate card must reconcile all transactions monthly. Failure to reconcile for two consecutive months may result in card suspension.
