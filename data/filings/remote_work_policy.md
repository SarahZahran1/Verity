# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines eligibility, expectations, and equipment provisions for employees working remotely at NimbusCloud Inc.

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to apply for remote work arrangements. Eligibility is reviewed by the employee's manager and HR business partner.

## Work Hours and Availability
Remote employees must be available during core collaboration hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their assigned time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may adjust their schedule with manager approval.

## Equipment Provision
The company provides a laptop, a docking station, and a one-time $500 home office stipend for eligible remote employees. Requests for additional equipment must be submitted through the IT Asset Request Portal.

## Security Requirements
Remote employees must use company-issued VPN software when accessing internal systems and must not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks for work involving customer data.

## Review Cycle
Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually during the employee's performance review cycle. Management reserves the right to modify or revoke remote work privileges based on performance or business needs.
