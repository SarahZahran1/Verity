# Information Security Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes minimum security requirements for all NimbusCloud Inc. employees and contractors accessing company systems.

## Password Requirements
All passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters, include at least one number and one special character, and must be rotated every 180 days. Password reuse across the last 10 passwords is prohibited.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all access to production systems, the internal VPN, and the company's cloud infrastructure console. Employees who disable MFA without IT Security approval are subject to immediate access revocation.

## Data Classification
Data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Restricted. Restricted data (including customer PII and payment information) may only be stored in approved, encrypted storage systems and may never be transferred via personal email or unencrypted USB drives.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including phishing attempts, lost devices, or unauthorized access, must be reported to security@nimbuscloud.example within 1 hour of discovery via the #security-incidents Slack channel or the Security Hotline.

## Third-Party Access
Vendors and contractors requiring system access must undergo a security review and sign a Data Processing Agreement before provisioning begins. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly.
