# Code of Conduct

## Purpose
This Code of Conduct sets the standard of professional behavior expected of all NimbusCloud Inc. employees, contractors, and partners.

## Respectful Workplace
Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind based on race, gender, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic will not be tolerated and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any financial or personal interest that could conflict with company decisions, including relationships with vendors, competitors, or direct reports. Disclosures are submitted to the Ethics Committee via the Conflict of Interest Form.

## Confidentiality
Employees are required to protect confidential company information, including unreleased product plans, financial results, and customer data, both during and after their employment with NimbusCloud Inc.

## Reporting Violations
Violations of this Code of Conduct may be reported anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics@nimbuscloud.example. Retaliation against anyone reporting a violation in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## Enforcement
Confirmed violations of this Code of Conduct are reviewed by HR and Legal, and disciplinary outcomes range from a formal written warning to immediate termination depending on severity.
